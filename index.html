Anyone can make a nice-looking video. I make the one that solves your problem.

It started with a GoPro my parents gave me in high school: beach days, summer montages, anything I could cut together. Now I use that same love of making things to help local people and businesses show what they do.

The camera is just the tool. The real work is listening, figuring out what you need the video to do, and making it happen.
